About Typing Hero

Typing Hero turns words into enemies: type each word correctly to defeat it before it reaches you. It blends vocabulary, accuracy, and speed pressure — the faster and cleaner you type, the longer you survive the escalating waves.

How to Play

  • Type the word shown on each approaching enemy.
  • Finish the word before the enemy reaches you.
  • Errors break your flow — accuracy protects you.
  • Survive waves of increasing speed and word length.

Tips & Strategy

  • Lock onto one word at a time; never split attention mid-word.
  • Short words are quick kills — clear them first under pressure.
  • Keep fingers on home row so resets cost no time.
