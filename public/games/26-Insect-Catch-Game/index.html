About Insect Catch

Insect Catch is a quick-hands catching game. Insects dart across the screen and your catcher has to intercept them before they escape. The faster they come, the more anticipation matters — chasing from behind is a losing strategy.

How to Play

  • Move your catcher to intercept insects.
  • Catch each insect before it crosses the screen.
  • Missed insects cost you — keep escapes to a minimum.
  • Score grows with every catch and streak.

Tips & Strategy

  • Move to where the insect is going, not where it is.
  • Stay central to halve your travel distance to either edge.
  • Streaks multiply score — protect them over single risky catches.
