About Hangman

Hangman is the classic word-guessing game. A hidden word waits behind blanks; guess letters one at a time. Correct letters reveal themselves everywhere they appear — wrong ones add a stroke to the gallows. Run out of strokes and the game is lost.

How to Play

  • Guess one letter at a time.
  • Correct letters appear in every matching blank.
  • Wrong guesses add to the drawing — you have a limited number.
  • Reveal the whole word before the drawing is complete to win.

Tips & Strategy

  • Start with vowels: A, E, I, O, U appear in most words.
  • E is the most common letter in English; T and A follow.
  • Short words are the hardest — guess common consonants (R, S, T, L, N) early.
