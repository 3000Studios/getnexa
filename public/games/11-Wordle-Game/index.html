How to play

Guess the hidden word in a limited number of tries.

Each guess must be a valid word. Type with the keyboard or tap the on-screen keys, then press Enter.

After each guess, the tiles show:

  • W Green — letter is in the word and in the correct spot.
  • E Yellow — letter is in the word but in the wrong spot.
  • A Gray — letter is not in the word.

Choose a level: Easy (4 letters), Medium (5), Hard (6), or Expert (7). Turn on Show hints for a small clue about the word.

About Wordle

Wordle is the daily word-deduction game. Guess a five-letter word in six tries; each guess colors its letters — green for right letter and place, yellow for right letter wrong place, gray for not in the word. It is a constraint puzzle disguised as a guessing game.

How to Play

  • Type a five-letter word and submit your guess.
  • Green = correct letter in the correct spot.
  • Yellow = the letter is in the word but in another spot.
  • Use every clue to narrow the next guess; six tries total.

Tips & Strategy

  • Open with a vowel-rich word like CRANE, RAISE, or SLATE.
  • Make your second guess test entirely new letters.
  • Never reuse gray letters — they are proven dead ends.
