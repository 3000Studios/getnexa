About Quiz Master

Quiz Master tests your knowledge across categories with rapid questions. Every correct answer scores; streaks raise the stakes. It is trivia in its purest arcade form — broad knowledge and fast recall beat deep expertise in a single subject.

How to Play

  • Read each question and choose an answer.
  • Correct answers score; streaks add bonuses.
  • Questions span many categories.
  • Answer as many correctly as you can before the end.

Tips & Strategy

  • Eliminate two wrong answers first if you are unsure.
  • Trust your first instinct on factual recall questions.
  • Streaks matter — don't gamble a streak on a wild guess late.
