About Tilting Maze

Tilting Maze rolls a ball through a labyrinth by tilting the whole board. Momentum is your enemy and your tool: tilt gently to build control, catch the ball before corners, and thread it to the exit without dropping into a trap hole.

How to Play

  • Tilt the board to roll the ball.
  • Navigate corridors to reach the exit.
  • Avoid trap holes scattered through the maze.
  • Reach the goal to complete the level.

Tips & Strategy

  • Feather the tilt — full tilt means full speed and no steering.
  • Stop the ball before each turn instead of swinging through it.
  • Plan two corridors ahead; momentum punishes improvisation.
