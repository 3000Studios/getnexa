About Chess

Chess, the classic game of strategy, playable against a friend on the same screen or against the built-in AI. Capture the enemy king's safety away move by move: control the center, develop your pieces, and think in tactics — forks, pins, and skewers decide most games at every level.

How to Play

  • Choose Vs Friend (same screen) or Vs AI.
  • Move pieces by their legal patterns; capture by landing on an enemy piece.
  • Checkmate the enemy king — threaten it with no escape — to win.
  • Castle early to protect your king and activate your rook.

Tips & Strategy

  • Control the center squares with pawns and pieces in the opening.
  • Develop knights and bishops before launching attacks.
  • Before every move, check what your opponent threatens.
