About Crossy Road

Crossy Road is the endless hopper: hop forward across roads, rivers, and rail lines without getting hit, swept away, or left behind by the scrolling screen. Traffic comes in patterns — reading the density of a lane before you commit is the core skill.

How to Play

  • Hop forward, left, right, or back one square at a time.
  • Cross roads without touching vehicles.
  • Ride logs across rivers — standing in water ends the run.
  • Keep moving; the screen scrolls and dawdling is fatal.

Tips & Strategy

  • Wait for a vehicle to pass completely before hopping into its lane.
  • On rivers, count the logs before jumping — patience beats bravery.
  • Stay near the center of roads where you have escape routes both ways.
