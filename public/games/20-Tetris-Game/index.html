About Nexa Tetris

Nexa Tetris is the timeless falling-block puzzle. Seven tetromino shapes fall one at a time; rotate and place them to complete horizontal lines, which clear and score. The fall speed rises as you clear lines, and the game ends when the stack reaches the top.

How to Play

  • Move pieces left and right as they fall.
  • Rotate pieces to fit gaps in your stack.
  • Complete a full horizontal line to clear it.
  • Clear up to four lines at once (a Tetris) for the biggest score.

Tips & Strategy

  • Keep your stack flat — bumps and holes cause cascades of trouble.
  • Save the long I-piece for a Tetris instead of spending it on singles.
  • Look at the next-piece preview and plan one piece ahead.
