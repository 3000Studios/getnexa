About Nexa Snake

Nexa Snake is a neon take on the classic Snake formula. Eat pellets to grow longer and raise your score, but every segment you add makes the arena more dangerous — one collision with the wall or your own tail ends the run. Unlockable skins reward long-term high scores.

How to Play

  • Guide the snake with arrow keys, WASD, or swipe.
  • Eat pellets to grow and score.
  • Avoid walls and your own tail.
  • The longer you get, the tighter the turns become.

Tips & Strategy

  • Hug the walls early to keep the center open for escapes.
  • Plan your route to the pellet before you turn, not after.
  • When in doubt, take the longer safe loop instead of the shortcut.
