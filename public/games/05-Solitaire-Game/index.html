About Solitaire

Solitaire is the classic Klondike card game. Build four foundation piles from Ace to King by suit while organizing the tableau in descending alternating colors. Every face-down card you uncover opens new options — patience and move order matter more than luck.

How to Play

  • Move cards between tableau columns in descending, alternating-color order.
  • Build foundations up from Ace to King by suit.
  • Flip the stock for new cards when no moves remain.
  • Move all cards to the foundations to win.

Tips & Strategy

  • Uncover face-down cards before playing stock cards.
  • Move Aces and 2s to foundations right away.
  • Don't empty a tableau column unless you have a King to fill it.
