About Fruit Slicer

Fruit Slicer sends fruit arcing across the screen and gives you a blade. Swipe through fruit to slice it for points; chain multiple fruit in one swipe for combos, and never — ever — slice the bomb. Speed and restraint in equal measure.

How to Play

  • Swipe across fruit to slice it.
  • Slice several fruit in one swipe for combo points.
  • Avoid bombs — slicing one is costly.
  • Missed fruit that falls away costs you.

Tips & Strategy

  • Wait for fruit to cluster before a big combo swipe.
  • Short, controlled swipes slice cleaner than wild slashes.
  • Bombs often hide behind fruit — check before you swipe.
