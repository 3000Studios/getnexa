About Typing Pro

Typing Pro is the advanced typing test: longer passages, punctuation, capitalization, and a full breakdown of speed and accuracy. It is built for typists who have outgrown beginner drills and want an honest professional benchmark.

How to Play

  • Type the full passage exactly, including punctuation.
  • Your WPM and accuracy update live.
  • Errors are tracked — backspace discipline matters.
  • Finish the passage for your complete results.

Tips & Strategy

  • Read a few words ahead of your fingers at all times.
  • Slow down on punctuation clusters; they are where accuracy dies.
  • Benchmark weekly, not daily — real gains show over weeks.
