About Connect Four

Connect Four drops discs into a seven-column grid; first player to line up four of their discs — across, down, or diagonally — wins. The tactics are real: control the center column, build threats your opponent must answer, and never hand them the winning square.

How to Play

  • Drop one disc per turn into any column.
  • Discs stack from the bottom of the column.
  • Connect four of your discs in any direction to win.
  • Block your opponent's three-in-a-row before it becomes four.

Tips & Strategy

  • The center column is the strongest square on the board — fight for it.
  • Create two threats at once; only one can be blocked.
  • Check diagonals before every drop — they are the sneakiest wins.
