About Tic-Tac-Toe

Tic-Tac-Toe on NEXA supports real-time two-player matches with a shareable room code. The rules are the ones everyone knows — three in a row wins — but against a live opponent the game becomes about forks: creating two winning threats at once so only one can be blocked.

How to Play

  • Create or join a room with a code to play a friend.
  • Take turns placing X and O on the 3×3 grid.
  • First to three in a row, column, or diagonal wins.
  • Block your opponent's two-in-a-row before it becomes three.

Tips & Strategy

  • Take the center first when you can — it appears in the most lines.
  • Watch for forks: a move that threatens two lines at once wins games.
  • If your opponent takes a corner, the center is the correct reply.
