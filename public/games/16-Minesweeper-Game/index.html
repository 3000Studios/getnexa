About Minesweeper

Minesweeper is the definitive logic puzzle. Click squares to reveal them: numbers tell you exactly how many mines touch that square, and blanks open up whole regions. One click on a mine ends the game. Almost every board is solvable by pure deduction — guessing is a last resort.

How to Play

  • Click a square to reveal it; start anywhere, corners open big areas.
  • Numbers show how many adjacent squares hide mines.
  • Flag squares you have proven are mines.
  • Clear every safe square without detonating a mine to win.

Tips & Strategy

  • Learn the 1-2-1 and 1-2 patterns — they solve most boards.
  • Flag confirmed mines immediately so you never misclick them.
  • Count mines section by section instead of treating the board as one problem.
