About Whack-A-Mole

Whack-A-Mole is the carnival classic: moles pop out of holes for a split second and your mallet has to be faster. Reflexes rule, but so does restraint — in harder moments, not everything that pops up should be whacked.

How to Play

  • Whack moles as they pop out of their holes.
  • Be fast — they duck back down quickly.
  • Chain hits to build your score before time runs out.
  • Avoid decoys or bombs where they appear.

Tips & Strategy

  • Keep your cursor (or finger) hovering at the center of the grid.
  • Use peripheral vision; focusing on one hole blinds you to the rest.
  • Rhythm beats fury — steady hits outscore wild swinging.
