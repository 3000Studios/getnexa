About 2048

2048 is the sliding-tile number puzzle that defined a genre. Swipe to slide every tile on the 4×4 grid; when two tiles with the same number collide they merge into one tile worth double. The goal is to build a 2048 tile — and strong players keep going to 4096 and beyond.

How to Play

  • Swipe or use arrow keys to slide all tiles in one direction.
  • Matching numbers merge into a single doubled tile.
  • A new tile (2 or 4) appears after every move.
  • The game ends when the grid fills with no merges left.

Tips & Strategy

  • Keep your biggest tile locked in one corner and never move it out.
  • Build a descending chain of values leading into that corner.
  • Avoid swiping in the direction that breaks your chain unless forced.
