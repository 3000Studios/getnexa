About Doodle Jump

Doodle Jump is the endless vertical jumper. Your character bounces automatically from platform to platform; you steer left and right (the screen wraps around) and climb as high as possible. Springs, jetpacks, and propeller hats rocket you upward — one missed platform ends the run.

How to Play

  • Steer left and right; your jumper bounces on its own.
  • Land on platforms to keep climbing.
  • Grab springs and power-ups for big boosts.
  • Avoid monsters and broken platforms; falling ends the run.

Tips & Strategy

  • Solid platforms are always safe — prioritize them over risky springs.
  • Jetpacks are the best power-up; never fly past one.
  • Aim slightly left of center when unsure — layouts tend to open left.
