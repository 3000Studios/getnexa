About Voice Guess

Voice Guess flips the number-guessing game to your microphone: say your guess out loud and the game listens. It is the same higher-or-lower deduction duel, played hands-free — clear speech and smart halving strategy win it.

How to Play

  • Speak your number guess into the microphone.
  • Listen for the higher-or-lower clue.
  • Narrow the range and guess again.
  • Find the hidden number in as few spoken guesses as you can.

Tips & Strategy

  • Speak slowly and clearly — enunciation beats volume.
  • Halve the range every guess, exactly like the typed version.
  • Play somewhere quiet; background noise confuses recognition.
