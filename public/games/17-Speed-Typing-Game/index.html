About Speed Typing

Speed Typing measures your words per minute against the clock. Type the presented text exactly — errors cost more than hesitation. It is the fastest honest mirror of your keyboard skill, and regular short sessions measurably raise real-world typing speed.

How to Play

  • Type the shown words exactly as they appear.
  • The timer tracks your words per minute (WPM) and accuracy.
  • Errors slow you down — correct flow beats raw speed.
  • Finish the passage to see your score.

Tips & Strategy

  • Accuracy first: speed follows clean typing automatically.
  • Keep your eyes on the text, never on your hands.
  • Use the home row and let muscle memory build — 10 minutes a day works.
