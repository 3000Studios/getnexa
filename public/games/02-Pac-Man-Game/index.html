About Pac-Man

Pac-Man is the maze chase that started it all. Clear every dot in the maze while four ghosts — each with its own hunting behavior — try to cut you off. Power pellets flip the chase for a few seconds, letting you eat the ghosts for bonus points.

How to Play

  • Guide Pac-Man through the maze eating dots.
  • Avoid Blinky, Pinky, Inky, and Clyde — each hunts differently.
  • Eat a power pellet to turn the ghosts blue and edible.
  • Clear all dots to advance to the next, faster level.

Tips & Strategy

  • Blinky chases you directly; Pinky aims ahead of you — cut corners to shake them.
  • Save power pellets until several ghosts are close for maximum points.
  • Learn one safe route per maze section and reuse it under pressure.
