About Memory Match

Memory Match is the classic concentration card game. A grid of face-down cards hides matching pairs; flip two at a time and remember what you saw. Clear the whole board in as few flips as possible. It is a pure test of short-term memory and systematic scanning.

How to Play

  • Flip any two cards to reveal them.
  • If they match, the pair stays cleared.
  • If not, both flip back — remember their positions.
  • Clear every pair to finish the board.

Tips & Strategy

  • Work through the board in a fixed order so locations stick in memory.
  • Say card positions out loud (or in your head) as you reveal them.
  • When you find one half of a pair, recall before you flip randomly.
