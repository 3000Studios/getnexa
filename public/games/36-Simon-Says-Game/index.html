About Simon Says

Simon Says is the memory pattern game: watch the sequence of lights and sounds, then repeat it back. Every round adds one more step. The game is a direct measurement of your sequence memory — and it trains exactly that, round by round.

How to Play

  • Watch the sequence of colors and tones.
  • Repeat the sequence in the same order.
  • Each round extends the sequence by one step.
  • One wrong step ends the run — so focus compounds.

Tips & Strategy

  • Give each color a verbal label in your head as it lights.
  • Chunk long sequences into groups of three or four.
  • Tap at a steady tempo; rushing scrambles recall.
