About Dice Roll

Dice Roll is a clean dice simulator for board games, tabletop sessions, and quick decisions. Roll single or multiple dice with fair, random results and running totals — a pocket dice cup that never loses a die under the couch.

How to Play

  • Choose how many dice to roll.
  • Tap to roll and see each die's result.
  • Totals are calculated automatically.
  • Re-roll any time for a fresh result.

Tips & Strategy

  • Use it for board-game nights when physical dice go missing.
  • Two-dice totals cluster around 7 — handy for probability intuition.
  • Keep a running tally feature in mind for score-based games.
