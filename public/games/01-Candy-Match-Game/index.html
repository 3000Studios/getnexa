About Candy Match

Candy Match is a match-3 puzzle game. Swap adjacent candies to line up three or more of a kind, clear them from the board, and trigger cascades as new candies fall into place. Bigger matches and chain reactions score far more than plain triples.

How to Play

  • Swap two adjacent candies to make a line of three or more.
  • Matched candies clear and new ones fall from above.
  • Chain reactions multiply your score.
  • Chase special matches of four or five for board-clearing effects.

Tips & Strategy

  • Set up matches near the bottom — cascades above them score free points.
  • A match of four is worth more than two separate triples.
  • Scan the whole board before swapping; the best move is rarely the first one you see.
