Control: Player Left(W and S) | Player Right(↑ and ↓)

About Ping Pong

Ping Pong brings table tennis to the arcade: fast rallies, sharp angles, and a paddle that rewards anticipation over reaction. Angle your returns off the paddle's edge to wrong-foot the other side, and expect the pace to climb with every exchange.

How to Play

  • Move your paddle to meet the ball.
  • Return it over the net line past your opponent.
  • Use paddle edges for angled shots.
  • Score by forcing a miss; first to the target wins.

Tips & Strategy

  • Recover to center after every return.
  • Watch the ball's angle off your paddle, not just its speed.
  • A defensive lob buys time to reset your position.
