About The Aviator

The Aviator is a cinematic flight experience: a stylized plane over open water and sky, flown with smooth, sweeping controls. It is the calmest seat in the arcade — a flight simulator about feel, altitude, and graceful lines rather than combat.

How to Play

  • Steer your plane with mouse, touch, or keys.
  • Climb, dive, and bank through open sky.
  • Explore the seascape at your own pace.
  • Enjoy a score-free flight — this one is about the flying.

Tips & Strategy

  • Make gentle inputs; smooth banks keep your line graceful.
  • Climb for the view, dive for the speed.
  • Treat it as the arcade's cooldown between high-score runs.
