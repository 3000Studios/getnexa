About Rock Paper Scissors

Rock Paper Scissors, the ultimate duel, against the computer. Rock crushes scissors, scissors cut paper, paper covers rock. Against a thinking opponent it becomes a psychology game: humans are predictable, and the player who spots the pattern first wins the series.

How to Play

  • Pick rock, paper, or scissors each round.
  • Rock beats scissors, scissors beats paper, paper beats rock.
  • Matching picks tie the round.
  • Win the series by reading your opponent's habits.

Tips & Strategy

  • People repeat winning picks and switch after losing — exploit both.
  • The most common opening pick worldwide is rock.
  • Random play is unbeatable in theory; patterned play is not.
