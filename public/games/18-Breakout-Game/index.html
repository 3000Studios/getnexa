About Brick Breaker

Brick Breaker is a glow-lit evolution of Breakout. Bounce a ball off your paddle to smash rows of bricks, chaining rebounds into combos. Miss the ball and you lose it — angle control off the paddle is the whole skill of the game.

How to Play

  • Move the paddle to keep the ball in play.
  • Bounce the ball into bricks to break them.
  • Clear every brick to advance.
  • Use paddle position to control the ball's rebound angle.

Tips & Strategy

  • Hit with the paddle's edge for sharp angles, center for straight returns.
  • Break a tunnel to the top so the ball ricochets above the bricks.
  • Don't chase the ball — position early and let it come to you.
