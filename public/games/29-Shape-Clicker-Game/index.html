About Shape Clicker

Shape Clicker flashes shapes and asks for one thing: click the right one, fast. Shapes that match the target score; wrong clicks and hesitation cost you. It is a pure test of visual discrimination under time pressure.

How to Play

  • Watch for the target shape or color.
  • Click matching shapes as they appear.
  • Avoid clicking anything else.
  • Score as many correct clicks as the clock allows.

Tips & Strategy

  • Fix your eyes on the center and use peripheral vision.
  • Commit quickly — a fast right answer beats a slow perfect one.
  • Wrong clicks hurt more than a beat of patience on ambiguous shapes.
