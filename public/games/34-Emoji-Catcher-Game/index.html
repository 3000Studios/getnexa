About Emoji Catcher

Emoji Catcher drops emojis from the sky and puts the basket in your hands. Catch the falling emojis to score, dodge the ones that cost you, and keep up as the fall speed climbs. Position and anticipation again — the eternal catching-game skills.

How to Play

  • Move the basket left and right under falling emojis.
  • Catch emojis to score points.
  • Avoid penalty items that cost points or lives.
  • Survive as the drop speed increases.

Tips & Strategy

  • Park under clusters, not single stragglers.
  • Small movements only — overrunning a drop is the classic miss.
  • Watch the top of the screen to plan your next position early.
