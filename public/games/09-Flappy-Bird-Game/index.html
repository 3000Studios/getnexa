About Flappy Bird

Flappy Bird is the one-button phenomenon. Tap to flap, fall when you don't, and thread your bird through the gap in every pipe. It looks random but scores are about rhythm: a steady tapping cadence carries you through most gaps without frantic corrections.

How to Play

  • Tap or click to flap upward.
  • Gravity pulls you down between taps.
  • Fly through each pipe gap without touching anything.
  • Every gap cleared scores a point; one touch ends the run.

Tips & Strategy

  • Find a rhythm and keep it — tap in a steady pattern rather than reacting.
  • Aim for the vertical center of each gap.
  • Take a short break after several quick deaths to reset your timing.
