About Tower Blocks

Tower Blocks is a timing-and-precision stacker. A crane swings blocks back and forth above your tower; drop each one so it lands as squarely as possible on the block below. Whatever overhangs gets trimmed away — so every imperfect drop makes the next one harder.

How to Play

  • A block swings above the tower — tap to drop it.
  • Landed overhang is trimmed off, shrinking your block.
  • Stack as high as you can before a block misses entirely.
  • Perfect drops keep your full block width.

Tips & Strategy

  • Watch the swing rhythm for two passes before dropping.
  • A slightly early drop beats a late one at higher speeds.
  • After a trim, slow down — the next block is smaller and less forgiving.
